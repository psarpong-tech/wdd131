Who we are

About EmpowerTech Girls Foundation

We believe every girl should have the encouragement and opportunity to explore technology and discover what she can create.

Our purpose

Making Technology Feel Possible

Technology affects how people learn, communicate, work, and solve problems. Yet some girls may have limited access to equipment, guidance, learning opportunities, or supportive communities.

EmpowerTech Girls Foundation is a developing initiative focused on helping girls ages 10–17 explore technology through learning, mentorship, and access to resources.

This website introduces the initiative's intended program areas and explains how families and supporters may get involved as plans develop.

Our direction

Mission, Vision, and Goals

01

Our Mission

To encourage girls ages 10–17 to explore technology by promoting digital learning, practical skills, mentorship, and access to educational resources.

02

Our Vision

A future where girls can confidently participate in technology, contribute ideas, and pursue opportunities in the digital world.

03

Our Goals

Introduce digital skills, encourage coding and problem-solving, connect learners with supportive mentors, and explore opportunities for learning support.

Who we hope to serve

Girls, Families, and Communities

Girls ages 10–17: learners who want to discover technology, develop practical skills, and build confidence.

Parents and guardians: families seeking information about safe, supportive learning opportunities for their children.

Volunteers and mentors: people willing to share knowledge, encouragement, and experience.

Schools and organizations: potential partners interested in supporting technology education and girls' participation.
